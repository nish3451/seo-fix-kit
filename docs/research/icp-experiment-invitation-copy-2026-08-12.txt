5. IF THEY ASK (answer truthfully, then log)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Can you audit my whole site?" → Full multi-page audits are in private beta,
invite/email-link gated; I can send an access link if you want one.

"What does the paid version cost?" → The Fix Pack is a $99.00 one-time
proof-backed repair pass tied to one report, plus one rerun after fixes; Dodo
checkout is the final price at payment time. It's offered only when a report
shows real fixes.

"You flagged X as critical but it's not broken!" → If it's the known 522/523
same-origin false critical or a fabricated snippet: "You're right — that's a
known false positive on our side, it has an owner and a fix in progress. Logging
it, thank you." Do NOT defend it. Record the objection.

"I already use [Ahrefs/Semrush/Screaming Frog/other]" → "Totally fair — those are
great for keywords/backlinks. The only thing I'd claim is rendered proof: what a
real browser sees vs what a static crawler sees. If that's not your problem, no
worries."

"I don't care about crawler false positives" → Record verbatim in the prospect
log. This is a keep/kill signal for the ICP definition.
