# Spec: Fix Execution And Staged Offers

## Goal

Move SEO Fix Kit from "proof-backed reports plus a manual Fix Pack" into a staged repair business that can honestly sell execution first, then monitoring, repair sprints, a recurring SEO/GEO repair agent, and an agency workspace.

## Product Position

SEO Fix Kit should not become a generic AI content factory. Outrank sells daily article generation, CMS publishing, Search Console-backed article refreshes, and backlink exchange. SEOitis sells AI-search visibility tracking plus gap-to-article-to-publish automation. Okara sells a broad AI CMO with SEO, GEO, social, content, analytics, CMS fixes, and GitHub-based coding work.

SEO Fix Kit's sharper lane is proof-backed repair. It should say: "We find the SEO and AI-search readiness problems that are actually visible, prove them, fix the safe ones, and rerun the proof."

## Competitor Benchmarks

- Outrank benchmark: daily SEO article production, automatic CMS publishing, Search Console-backed article improvements, and backlink exchange set the low-price content automation bar around a self-serve monthly plan.
- SEOitis benchmark: AI visibility tracking across multiple engines, prompt/citation scoring, SEO article generation, publishing, and full gap-to-article-to-measure loops justify higher subscription tiers.
- Okara benchmark: broad AI CMO positioning covers daily channel agents, SEO/GEO analytics, CMS fixes, GitHub PR fixes, and multi-channel content work.
- SEO Fix Kit implication: do not price against their content volume until SEO Fix Kit can execute fixes. Price first around repair value, proof quality, and reduced developer/agency coordination.

## Actors

- A1. Site owner: owns or controls a site and wants fixes prioritized by proof.
- A2. Founder/admin: operates Fix Pack fulfillment, pricing, delivery, and support.
- A3. Developer: receives repair instructions or reviews generated code/CMS changes.
- A4. Agency owner: manages multiple client reports and wants branded proof.
- A5. AI-search buyer: wants practical SEO/GEO readiness repairs without fake ranking guarantees.

## Requirements

### Foundation: Fix Execution First

- R1. Every paid repair offer must start from a saved report with proven issues, not from a generic checkout page.
- R2. The app must classify repair items by execution mode: safe in-app/CMS edit, GitHub PR candidate, customer/manual task, or unsupported.
- R3. The app must let the owner approve generated fixes before anything is applied or sent as final delivery.
- R4. Delivery must include before/after proof, original report, final rerun report, customer-visible notes, and no ranking guarantees.
- R5. Billing copy must stay truthful: payment buys one proof-backed repair pass and one rerun unless a later subscription entitlement is live.

### Offer 1: Monitoring / Reporting

- R6. A verified site owner can subscribe to recurring proof monitoring for one or more verified sites.
- R7. Monitoring must reuse existing audit schedules, report deltas, and saved report ownership.
- R8. Monitoring must avoid pretending to fix issues; it sells detection, proof, and change alerts.
- R9. Monitoring pricing should fit the lower tier: roughly $49-$99/month once billing entitlements exist.

### Offer 2: Repair Sprint

- R10. A site owner can buy a one-time repair sprint from a report with fixable issues.
- R11. A repair sprint must produce a scoped queue, owner approval, delivery status, and rerun proof.
- R12. A repair sprint may include manual delivery links, CMS edits, or GitHub PRs depending on integrations.
- R13. Repair sprint pricing should start around $249-$499 one-time once execution is visible and reliable.

### Offer 3: SEO/GEO Repair Agent

- R14. A verified site owner can enable a recurring repair agent that prioritizes SEO and AI-search readiness repairs from live proof.
- R15. The agent must prioritize by real opportunity signals: report severity, repeat issues, Search Console or imported keyword data, report deltas, and AI-search readiness checks.
- R16. The agent must include practical AI-search readiness checks without claiming Google needs llms.txt or special AI markup.
- R17. The agent must keep owner approval before publishing, merging, or marking delivery complete.
- R18. SEO/GEO repair agent pricing can move toward $199-$399/month after recurring execution is real.

### Offer 4: Agency Workspace

- R19. An agency owner can manage multiple client reports with branding, client links, PDF exports, issue assignment, and repair status.
- R20. Agency workspace must build on existing white-label reports, report domains, team members, and issue collaboration.
- R21. Agency workspace must separate client-facing proof from private admin notes and internal fulfillment state.
- R22. Agency pricing can move toward $299-$799/month after client/workspace limits and billing entitlements are implemented.

## Key Flows

- F1. Repair execution: report finds proven issues, owner starts a Fix Pack, system classifies fix modes, owner approves, admin or integration applies changes, final rerun proves the result.
- F2. Monitoring: verified site owner schedules recurring audits, receives deltas, and upgrades to repair sprint only when new proven issues appear.
- F3. Repair sprint: owner buys a scoped sprint from a report, tracks work in the repair board, receives delivery notes and rerun proof.
- F4. SEO/GEO repair agent: recurring agent reviews proof, prioritizes fixes with performance or AI-readiness context, proposes changes, waits for approval, then records proof.
- F5. Agency workspace: agency brands client reports, shares proof, assigns issues, and optionally sells repair sprints per client.

## Non-Goals

- No rank, traffic, indexing, revenue, or citation guarantees.
- No broad AI CMO clone in this sequence.
- No daily article factory in this sequence.
- No proprietary backlink discovery in this sequence.
- No auto-publishing or auto-merging without owner approval.
- No public claim that completed 50,000-page rendered validation is live until large-crawl batches have page-level proof and merge readiness.
- No client-side provider secrets, CMS tokens, GitHub tokens, Dodo secrets, Cloudflare admin APIs, or private service credentials.

## Acceptance Examples

- AE1. Given a paid report has fixable title and description issues, when the owner starts a repair sprint, then the app shows generated fix proposals, approval state, delivery status, and final rerun proof.
- AE2. Given a verified site has weekly monitoring enabled, when the scheduled audit finds no change, then the customer sees stable monitoring without a fake urgent repair prompt.
- AE3. Given imported Search Console rows show page-two queries with impressions, when the repair agent prioritizes work, then those pages are prioritized above low-severity cosmetic issues.
- AE4. Given a Google AI guidance conflict around llms.txt, when SEO/GEO checks run, then the app frames llms.txt as optional for non-Google systems and keeps foundational crawlability/content/schema checks primary.
- AE5. Given an agency shares a client report, when the client opens the link, then they see branded proof and status without admin notes, private tokens, or internal billing state.

## Pricing Guardrails

- Current honest charge: $149-$249 one-time for a beta Fix Pack, with $249 as the preferred standard once delivery proof is smooth.
- Do not launch $199-$399/month until recurring repair proposals and approval workflows exist.
- Do not launch $299-$799/month agency pricing until workspace limits, branded reports, issue ownership, and client-safe proof are coherent.
- Treat low-cost competitors as pressure on content volume, not proof-backed execution. SEO Fix Kit can charge more only when it saves implementation or coordination work.

## Success Metrics

- At least one repair sprint can move from paid to delivered with final rerun proof and no manual database edits.
- At least one recurring monitor can produce a clean delta and a repair upsell only when a new proven issue exists.
- At least one SEO/GEO repair proposal can explain why the fix matters without making unsupported AI-search claims.
- At least one agency report can be shared with branded proof, issue status, and no private admin leakage.
